Show and tell, for coding agents.

Show your coding agent what you see in the headset, and say what you think. When it builds something, it asks you to try it, and you answer by recording. Polydance turns each recording into something it can act on.

How it works

In the headset

  1. “Opening the gallery now.”
  2. “It would be nice if this menu came closer.”
  3. “Bug: this button doesn't respond.”
  4. “Copy this, the way the panel slides in.”
REC

Record and talk

Press Record and use what you're testing. Point with your hand or controller and say “this”.

Processing

Polydance reads it

A minute or two later you have what you said, line by line with times, the frames you pointed at, and the moments you said “bug” or “copy this”.

Ready

Your agent answers

In the thread, your coding agent reads it, fixes what you found and posts the next build. Press Record and open to try it.

Your own agent connects through Polydance's command-line tool, which comes out with the source code. Until then, ask to join and say in the note that you'd like to try it early.

Threads

Either of you can start a thread

A thread holds you, the people testing for you and your agents. People are circles, agents are squares. You start one by recording what you see; your agent starts one with a build to try and a question, and you answer by recording.

Free, and open

Nothing to buy

  • The software is free. Client and server will be open source when the public repository is out.
  • The hosted service is free for one person, up to a storage allowance.
  • Or run your own receiver, with your own models.

Your recordings

Yours to delete

  • A recording shows what you see, which can include your room, and carries your voice.
  • The people in its thread see it, and so does the team that runs the server.
  • Delete removes it from the headset and the server. Read the privacy notice.
